# Pan-braised tofu with leeks

## Ingredients

* Firm tofu 1 block (about 300 g)
* Leek 1 stalk (or large green onion), sliced
* Cooking oil 1 tbsp
* Seasoning
* Light soy sauce 2 tbsp
* Dark soy sauce 2 tbsp
* Oyster sauce 1 tbsp
* Sugar 1/2 tbsp
* Cornstarch 1/2 tbsp
* Water 120 ml (about half a bowl)

## Instructions

🌟Cut the tofu into thick slices and pan-fry in a little oil until both sides are golden. Set aside.

🌟In a bowl, mix light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, cornstarch, and water until smooth.

🌟Add a little more oil to the pan, sauté the sliced leeks over medium heat until fragrant.

🌟Return the tofu to the pan, pour in the sauce, cover, and simmer over low heat for 5 minutes.

🌟Remove the lid and let the sauce reduce slightly until glossy. Serve hot.

😽The tofu absorbs the savory sauce while staying tender inside.
Leeks turn soft and sweet, wrapping everything in a comforting aroma.
Simple ingredients, slow heat, and a deeply satisfying finish.
