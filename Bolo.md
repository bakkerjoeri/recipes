# Bolognese

## Ingredients for 4 servings
- 300 grams of minced meats, I prefer a mix of beef and pork
- 150 grams of pork sausage, broken up into tiny pieces
- 100 grams of pancetta or, if you can't find it, smoked porkbelly, diced
- 2 yellow onions, diced
- 3 cloves of garlic, diced
- 1 big carrot, diced
- 3 sticks of celery, diced
- 1 can of tomato puree (double concentrate, unsalted)
- 1 cup of red or white wine
- 1 cup of broth (your choice)
- 1 cup of milk
- 1 tsp fennel seeds
- A bunch of fresh herbs, I like laurel, thyme and rosemary

## Instructions

1. In butter and oil on medium fire sauté the onion. Once glassy add the carrot and celery and a dash of salt and slowly cook until they start to brown a bit. Remove from the pan and set aside.
2. In the same pan toss in the fennel seeds to toast and start adding in the sausage bits. Then add the bacon or pancetta. Once those are nice and browned increase the fire and add the mince, season with salt and pepper. You want the mince to brown, not just cook, so don't be afraid to let it sit and don't stir too often. The meat and vegetables should now have formed a nice fond at the bottom of the pan.
3. Add the vegetables back in, lower the fire a bit.
5. Add the tomato puree, stir through and let it bake a bit
6. Use the wine to deglaze. Cook until the alcohol is gone
7. Add the broth and the milk and the herbs and bring to a simmer
8. Lower the heat, cover enough to prevent splashing but keep open enough to let it reduce. Let it sit at least 45 minutes, but the longer the better
9. Cook the pasta to al dente. Then add it to the sauce, make sure to scoop along a little bit of pasta fluid

Serve with parm and maybe basil
